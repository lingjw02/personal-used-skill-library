# Example: Full-Body Illustration (footroom, pose, costume flow)

An original character. This example emphasizes what changes when the target is **full-body**: footwear must be resolved, pose anatomy matters more, and cloth flow needs one coherent direction.

**User request:** "I only have a cropped waist-up reference of my wind-courier character. I want a full-body hero illustration. Midjourney."

---

## Reference observation (summary)

- Young adult, athletic, ~7-head proportions
- Short copper-red hair, swept back, with **two long thin braids tied with white cloth strips** trailing behind
- Sharp, upturned green eyes; confident smirk; a small scar across the left eyebrow
- Costume: sleeveless cropped cream vest with brown leather straps crossing the chest; **oversized teal scarf wrapped twice and streaming to one side**; fingerless leather gloves; fitted bracers
- Lower body, footwear, and any belt or pack are **not visible** in the reference
- Palette: warm copper + cream + brown, with teal scarf as the strong accent; sunny warm temperature

## Uncertain features (critical for this request)

| Feature | Why unknown | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| Trousers / lower garment | cropped | Conservative: fitted dark-brown riding trousers, plain |
| Footwear | cropped | Conservative: simple laced leather boots, brown |
| Belt / pack | cropped | Omit pack; add a plain leather belt so the lower half connects to the straps |

These are **assumptions**, not identity. Tell the user so they can substitute their own design instead of unknowingly adopting invented details.

## Critical Identity List

1. Short swept-back copper-red hair with two long braids tied with white cloth strips
2. Sharp, upturned green eyes and a small scar across the left eyebrow
3. Oversized teal scarf wrapped twice, streaming to one side
4. Cream cropped vest with crossed brown leather chest straps
5. Fingerless leather gloves and bracers
6. Warm copper/cream/brown palette with teal accent

## What changes vs. reference

Static waist-up view → dynamic mid-stride pose on a windswept ridge; camera moved to a slight low angle so the scarf and braids fill negative space. The scarf and braids both stream in the **same wind direction** (behind-left), which also reinforces the silhouette.

## Full-body specifics applied

- **Footroom:** "feet and boots fully visible with ground contact" is stated in CAMERA and COMPOSITION.
- **Pose anatomy:** mid-stride with weight forward on the right leg, left leg trailing, torso leaning slightly into the wind; hands relaxed (gloved) rather than complex gestures.
- **Cloth flow:** one wind direction shared by scarf, braids, and vest hem.
- **Detail budget:** Tier 3 leather-stitching and buckle details compressed to a single clause; boots kept simple.
